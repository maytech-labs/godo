## Unreleased

## 0.1.0 - 2026-11-05

- First release: a pet that lives in your terminal and keeps living while
  the app is closed.
- Feed, clean, hug, medicine, and lights out; grows from egg to adult over a
  week.
- Five minigames: Which Way?, Snack Rain, Hop!, Echo, and High or Low.
- `termigatchi status` for your shell prompt, plus `feed`, `clean`, `hug`,
  and `rename` from any shell.
